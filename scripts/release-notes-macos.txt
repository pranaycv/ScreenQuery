Test builds of ScreenQuery.

- ScreenQuery-arm64.dmg — Apple silicon (M1 or later). About This Mac says Chip: Apple M1, M2, M3, or M4.
- ScreenQuery-x86_64.dmg — Intel Mac.
- ScreenQuery.exe — Windows 10 or later. Double-click it. No installer.

Hold Shift and press S and P together to capture the front window. Hold Shift and press S and O together to drag a rectangle. Shift+S alone does nothing.

Drag ScreenQuery to Applications, eject the disk image, then Control-click the app and choose Open. This build is not notarized, so Gatekeeper warns the first time. If macOS says the app is damaged, run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ScreenQuery.app

Settings opens in front. Allow Screen Recording and Input Monitoring, quit, and open ScreenQuery again. Hold Shift and press S and P.
